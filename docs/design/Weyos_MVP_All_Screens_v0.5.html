What changed in v0.5 — and the decision it still needs

The wrapper is built to reading (a): a conversational interface over the deterministic engine. Every claim it makes traces to a rule that actually fired. It reports the engine; it does not reason on its own. That is what keeps the wellness positioning, the banned-vocabulary rule and the decision trace intact.

Reading (b) — a generative advisor that answers open questions from its own knowledge — is a different product and is not drawn here. It needs legal review before it is estimated, because free generation cannot be held to the §6 vocabulary ban.

The quiet-day problem is the crux (W1). Principle 1 says nothing should reward opening the app; Pav's wrapper is the front door on every session. The reconciliation drawn here: the wrapper opens, states where you are, and then stops talking. It never greets, never prompts, never asks how you are unless you speak first.

Still open: does the wrapper replace the A1–A11 form onboarding (W7 shows the progressive alternative Pav asked about), and does it ship in Phase 1 or 1.5? It does not fit inside 691h either way.
